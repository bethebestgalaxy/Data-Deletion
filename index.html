Account and Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play policies and GDPR privacy regulations, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your Business Star account and all associated personal and game data at any time.

1. How to Delete Your Account

Option A — Directly within the Game:

  1. Open the Business Star game.
  2. Tap your Profile / Avatar in the top-left corner of the main menu.
  3. Tap the red "Delete My Account" button.
  4. Confirm the prompt: your account and all data will be permanently wiped immediately.

Option B — Via Email (if the app is uninstalled):

Send an email to our support team using the link below, specifying your Player User ID or the email address linked to your Google Play account:

Support Email: be.the.best.galaxy@gmail.com

Send Request via Email

2. What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Unique player identifier (Firebase UID)
  • Username / Display name and profile avatar
  • Email address (if authenticated via Google)
  • Game progress (Level, XP, and Global League ranking)
  • Virtual currencies (In-game Coins and Gems)
  • Friends list, pending friend requests, and match invites

3. Data Retention & Processing Period

Account deletion requested within the app is instant and irreversible. For deletion requests sent via email, full removal of records from our Firebase database servers will be processed within a maximum of 7 business days.
